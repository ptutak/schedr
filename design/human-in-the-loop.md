## Current

### Approvals and questions

- **The master holds approval and question steps** (D283, planned (M6)): `approve` and `ask` declare
  an `ask` task with its question, answer schema, approvers and timeout. When ready it waits on the
  master for an answer, without running Python or occupying a worker or watcher slot.
- **Approval and question answers have typed outputs** (D284, planned (M6)): `approve` produces a
  yes-or-no answer; `ask` accepts a `Literal`, `Enum`, dataclass or pydantic model. The answer is
  the step's output and the master validates it against its schema.
- **A rejected approval has the Rejected outcome** (D285, planned (M6)): A no answer ends an
  approval with `Rejected`. `.on(Rejected)` branches on it, while tasks downstream through plain
  `>>` are skipped.
- **Approvers default to users who may trigger the DAG** (D286, planned (M6)): Anyone who may
  trigger the DAG can answer by default. `approvers` narrows access to users or groups, and
  `four_eyes=True` excludes the run's initiator.
- **Questions time out with the TimedOut outcome** (D287, planned (M6)): An ask step uses its
  `timeout`, or `[ask] default_timeout` when absent. The default is seven days; expiration produces
  `TimedOut`.
- **Pending questions appear in the UI, CLI and notifications** (D288, planned (M6)): The UI has a
  Waiting for you list, badge and task-panel answer form; the CLI provides `schedr runs answer`. A
  `WAITING_FOR_ANSWER` notification is sent through configured channels, with email linking to the
  task.
- **Answers are final and recorded in the command log** (D289, planned (M6)): Each answer records
  who answered, when, what they answered and an optional comment in the command log. The task panel
  shows the record; an answer is final.
